## [WARNING] The orthographic views omit hidden lines

The front, top and side views of this drawing show visible edges only. Hidden edges, which drawings usually show as dashed lines, are omitted from these views.

The absence of hidden lines is not evidence that the part is solid:

- A plain rectangular outline can still represent a hollow box, a frame or a block with a deep pocket.
- The absence of hidden lines does not, by itself, determine the depth of a pocket or hole or the thickness of a wall.

Cross-check geometry using:

- visible edges across the orthographic views and hidden edges shown in the isometric view;
- printed dimensions, following their dimension and extension lines to identify the faces or outlines they measure. A small nearby value alone does not establish wall thickness.

The comparison images leave hidden edges out as well. A perfect match alone does not confirm a depth or wall thickness that these views do not constrain.
